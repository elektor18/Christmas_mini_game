Privacy Policy

Christmas Arcade does not require an account. Game progress, settings, and virtual rewards are stored locally on your device. Advertising services described below may process personal data.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to manage banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Unity Ads is integrated as an advertising partner through AdMob mediation and may serve these advertisements when selected by AdMob. These services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, and information about ad delivery and interactions to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud, according to their privacy policies and applicable privacy choices. Unity may also share advertising-related information with its advertising and measurement partners as described in its privacy policy.

Where applicable, Google's consent message provides advertising privacy choices before ad requests. You can reopen these choices using the app's Privacy options button when available. Unity advertisements also provide a Data Privacy icon for Unity-specific choices, including opting out of personalized advertising, accessing your data, and requesting deletion.

Data Usage

  • No account creation
  • Gameplay progress and preferences are stored on the device
  • Advertising partners, including Unity Ads, may process device and ad interaction data
  • Saving or sharing artwork is initiated by the user through the selected device or sharing service
