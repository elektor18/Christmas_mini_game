01 / The essentials

A simple overview

Christmas Arcade does not require an account. Game progress, settings, and virtual rewards are stored locally on your device.

Advertising services described below may process personal data.

02 / Your data

Playing, saving & sharing

  • No account creation

    You can play Christmas Arcade without creating an account.

  • Game progress & preferences

    Your game progress, settings, and virtual rewards are saved locally on your device.

  • Saving & sharing artwork

    Saving or sharing artwork is initiated by you through the selected device or sharing service.

03 / Advertising

How advertising works

The app uses Google AdMob to manage banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Unity Ads is integrated as an advertising partner through AdMob mediation and may serve these advertisements when selected by AdMob.

Information advertising partners may process

  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • IP addresses and device information
  • Information about ad delivery and interactions

Advertising partners, including Unity Ads, may process this information to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud, according to their privacy policies and applicable privacy choices.

Unity may also share advertising-related information with its advertising and measurement partners as described in its privacy policy.

04 / Privacy choices

Your advertising choices

Privacy options in the app

Where applicable, Google's consent message provides advertising privacy choices before ad requests. You can reopen these choices using the app's Privacy options button when available.

Unity's Data Privacy icon

Unity advertisements also provide a Data Privacy icon for Unity-specific choices, including opting out of personalized advertising, accessing your data, and requesting deletion.
