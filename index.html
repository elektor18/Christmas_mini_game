This Privacy Policy explains how Christmas Arcade ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

The developer does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, postal address, phone number, contacts, photographs, or precise location.

However, the App uses third-party advertising services that may automatically collect or process certain technical information, including:

  • Device identifiers or other identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device type and operating system information
  • App interactions related to advertising
  • IP address or approximate location derived from the IP address
  • Advertising performance and diagnostic information

The exact information processed may depend on your device, region, privacy settings, and consent choices.

2. Advertising

Christmas Arcade contains advertisements.

The App uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to display advertising.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers and other technical information for purposes such as:

  • Delivering advertisements
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Preventing fraud and abuse
  • Providing contextual or personalised advertising where permitted
  • Improving advertising services

Where required by applicable law, users may be asked for consent before personalised advertising or related data processing is enabled.

If consent is not provided, non-personalised or limited advertising may be shown where supported.

3. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services provided by:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads
  • Google Play services and related Android platform services

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies and may process data independently from the developer.

The developer does not control the data-processing practices of third-party advertising providers.

Google Privacy Policy

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed through third-party services may be used to:

  • Display and deliver advertisements
  • Measure advertisement performance
  • Detect fraudulent or invalid advertising activity
  • Maintain the security and reliability of advertising services
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

The developer does not sell users' personal information.

5. Data Sharing

The App itself does not directly sell or trade users' personal information.

Certain information, including device or advertising identifiers, may be transmitted to or processed by advertising and technology providers as necessary to provide advertisements and related services.

Such processing may constitute data sharing under definitions used by Google Play or applicable privacy laws.

6. Data Storage

Christmas Arcade does not provide user accounts and the developer does not maintain a database containing users' personal information.

Game progress, preferences, scores, or settings may be stored locally on the user's device where required for the operation of the App.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to the retention and security practices of those service providers.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information transmitted through third-party services.

Third-party services used by the App may use encrypted connections when transmitting data. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Christmas Arcade is designed to provide family-friendly entertainment.

The developer does not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children.

Advertising and other third-party services may apply different data-processing rules depending on the user's age, region, consent status, and applicable laws.

Parents or guardians who believe that personal information relating to a child has been collected improperly may contact the developer using the details below.

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights regarding the processing of your personal information, including the right to:

10. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers may process information on servers located outside your country of residence.

Where required, appropriate safeguards are used by those providers for international transfers of personal information.
