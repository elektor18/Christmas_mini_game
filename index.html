Privacy Policy

Christmas Arcade does not require an account. Game progress, settings, and virtual rewards are stored locally on your device. Advertising services described below may process personal data.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to manage advertisements. Unity Ads can also serve advertisements through AdMob mediation. These services may process device and advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and information about ad delivery and interactions to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud, according to their privacy policies and applicable privacy choices.

Where applicable, Google's consent message provides advertising privacy choices before ad requests. You can reopen these choices using the app's Privacy options button when available.

Unity Ads through AdMob Mediation

Unity Ads is integrated as an advertising partner through Google AdMob mediation. AdMob manages ad requests and selects the advertising network that serves each ad. Unity Ads may provide banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements in the app.

When Unity Ads is used, Unity may process device and advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, and information about ad delivery and your interactions with advertisements. Unity may use this information for advertising, personalization where permitted by your privacy choices, measurement, and fraud prevention. Unity may share advertising-related information with its advertising and measurement partners as described in its privacy policy.

Unity advertisements provide a Data Privacy icon that lets you manage Unity-specific privacy choices, including opting out of personalized advertising, accessing your data, and requesting deletion. Details about Unity's data handling and your choices are available in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Data Usage

  • No account creation
  • Gameplay progress and preferences are stored on the device
  • Advertising partners, including Unity Ads, may process device and ad interaction data
  • Saving or sharing artwork is initiated by the user through the selected device or sharing service

  • Unity Ads (through AdMob mediation) / Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
